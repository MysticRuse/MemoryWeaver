💡 How Curation Works:
  • Where do photos come from? The curation system works only with the active photos you've chosen to include (completely ignoring any photos you hid in Step 2).
  • How does it pick the best photos (e.g. 50 photos)? Our smart system does all the heavy lifting for you! If you select "50 Photos," it reviews all your pictures, groups similar shots together (so you never get duplicates), filters out any blurry shots, and selects the absolute top 50 highest-scoring highlights. Your other photos are kept safe and untouched!
  • What does the size mean (50 vs 100)? Choosing "50 Photos" compiles your top 50 moments. Selecting a larger size (like 100 Photos) builds a longer, more detailed storybook. Because the system performs deep quality analysis on each photo to pick the perfect highlights, larger books take a few extra minutes to compile.
